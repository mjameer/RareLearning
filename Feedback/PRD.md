i like the doc, MJ! I have one recommendation: when asking about product requirements, specifically to product managers, instead of phrasing from a technical solution perspective, phrase from a product requirement perspective.
example, you asked:
	1) Should cloud service stores the distance setting for apps to access? Rex Wang 
Product managers should not be making technical solution suggestions. They can make product decisions informed by technical challenges and limitations and costs.
A better question is:
	Is it required to persist the distance setting? If so, should it be persisted at the account level? or is the temporal nature of on device sufficient enough?
 
This might feel pedantic, but I think changing that perspective really helps drive the requirement gathering process without muddling with solutions up front
